## What is FusionAL?

FusionAL is a **self-hosted MCP governance gateway** that sits between your AI clients and your tool servers. One endpoint. Central auth. Tool-level policy enforcement. Full audit trail. Deployable in a single Docker command.

**The problem:** Every MCP server you connect loads its full tool manifest into Claude's context window before processing a single token. At 84 tools across several servers, that's 15,540 tokens consumed at session start — before your team has asked anything. At scale, that's cost, latency, and an audit surface nobody can explain to legal.
